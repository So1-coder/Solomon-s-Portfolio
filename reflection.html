My Reflection

My IT Journey

My journey in Information Technology has allowed me to develop both my technical knowledge and my problem-solving skills. When I first started learning IT, I was interested in computers and technology, but I did not have a clear idea of all the different areas within the field.

Through my studies and practical work, I have gained experience with programming, databases, software development and version control. Working with Java has helped me understand programming concepts, while MySQL has introduced me to database design and SQL.

What I Have Learned

One of the most important things I have learned is that developing technical skills requires practice and patience. Some programming problems can be challenging, but working through errors has helped me become more confident in finding solutions.

I have also learned the importance of testing and checking my work. Using JUnit has shown me how testing can help identify problems in a program, while GitHub has helped me understand the importance of keeping track of changes to my projects.

My Future Goals

In the future, I would like to continue improving my programming skills and learn more about software development. I am also interested in cybersecurity and would like to develop my knowledge in this area.

I plan to continue building practical projects, learning new technologies and developing the skills needed for a successful career in Information Technology.
